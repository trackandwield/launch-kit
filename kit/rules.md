# Kit rules (every company, every asset)

These apply on top of the company's own brand rules. Where they conflict, these win.

1. **Facts come from two places only:** the brief and the product's `messaging-framework.md`. Anything else is not a fact.
2. **Name gaps, don't fill them.** If the brief has no date, proof, plan or CTA, the asset says so in a note to the reviewer instead of inventing one.
3. **Every claim is traceable.** The writer lists each claim with the brief field or framework line it came from.
4. **Limitations are part of the story.** If the brief lists one, assets that describe the feature mention it where a buyer would care.
5. **Disclaimer:** if the company's `company.yaml` has a `disclaimer`, every asset ends with it, word for word.
6. **Banned words** (on top of the brand's own list): revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, synergy, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, unleash, supercharge, "single pane of glass".
7. **No invented people:** no customer names, quotes or testimonials unless the brief supplies them with a source.
