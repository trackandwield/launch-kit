## Positioning statement

For **teams that work across time zones and calendars**, who **lose hours to meetings and long written updates that still miss the context**, Loom is **async video messaging** that **lets anyone record their screen and voice, share it in seconds, and turn it into the docs, tickets and notes the work needs**. Unlike **live meetings or text-only updates**, Loom **keeps the tone and visual context of showing someone, without everyone having to be there at once.**

## Messaging pillars

### 1. Show, don't schedule (`show_dont_schedule`)
- **Claim:** Replace a meeting with a short video people watch when it suits them.
- **Proof:** Record screen and camera; share with a link; comments and emoji reactions.
- **Pain it answers:** Calendars full of status meetings across time zones.

### 2. Video that becomes work (`video_becomes_work`)
- **Claim:** A recording turns into the doc, message or ticket that comes next.
- **Proof:** AI workflows turn a video into a document, message or Jira bug report; auto summaries and chapters.
- **Pain it answers:** Re-typing what you just explained on video.

### 3. Polished without editing skills (`polished_fast`)
- **Claim:** Videos look considered without an editor.
- **Proof:** Filler-word and silence removal, edit by transcript, overlays, auto titles.
- **Pain it answers:** Re-recording five times to get one clean take.

### 4. Built for the whole company (`company_ready`)
- **Claim:** Ready for IT and security to roll out.
- **Proof:** SSO and SCIM via Atlassian Guard Standard, custom data retention, 99.95% uptime SLA on Enterprise; part of Atlassian.
- **Pain it answers:** Shadow tools IT can't govern.

## Personas

| Persona | What they care about | What they need to hear | Words to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Team lead or manager** (`team_lead`, main buyer) | Fewer meetings, team alignment | Time back, updates people actually watch | "game-changing", vague productivity percentages |
| **Engineer or product manager** (`builder`, daily user) | Explaining bugs and designs fast | Bug reports into Jira, code and design walkthroughs | Marketing adjectives |
| **Customer-facing rep** (`customer_facing`) | Personal outreach and support that scales | Personal video, viewer insights, CTAs | "viral", "hack" |
| **IT admin** (`it_admin`, late stage) | Control, security, rollout | SSO, SCIM, retention, uptime | Anything not on the Enterprise list |
