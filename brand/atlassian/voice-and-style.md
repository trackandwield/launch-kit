# Atlassian voice and style rules (for the writer and reviewer agents)

Condensed from Atlassian's public design system content guidelines, read 2026-10-19:
- Voice and tone: https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/voice-tone
- Language and grammar: https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/language-and-grammar

Those pages cover product and UI writing. Atlassian's marketing voice guide is internal only, so marketing-specific rules here come from the public pages plus published Loom launch posts (see `style-references.md`). Paraphrased, not copied.

## Voice

Atlassian's voice is **bold, optimistic, and practical, with a wink**.

| Trait | Turn it up when | Turn it down when |
|---|---|---|
| Bold | Writing for confident, power users or admins | Introducing a new concept, or writing for trial users |
| Optimistic | Showing what's now possible | The reader is new, unsure or blocked |
| Practical, with a wink | Always practical. The wink only when the reader feels successful or relieved | The reader is stressed, stuck or overwhelmed: no wink |

## Tone principles to use in launch content

1. **Inform to build trust:** say clearly what's new and what it does, with next steps. Don't pad.
2. **Empower to inspire action:** offer best practices and how to decide. Assume a basic understanding; don't over-explain.
3. **Motivate by showing possibilities:** lead with the outcome and the end state, not the problem. Show how teams use it. Never list features without their benefit.
4. **Satisfy by meeting expectations:** give the answer, then get out of the way. Release notes and docs-style copy are practical only.
5. **Delight sparingly:** small celebratory touches at most once per asset; nothing that won't translate for a global audience.

## Style rules the reviewer checks

**Capitalization and names**
- Sentence case for every heading, title, button and label. Capitalize only the first word, proper nouns and product names.
- Full product and feature names in customer copy ("Jira Service Management", never "JSM").
- No "e.g.", "i.e.", "etc." or "&".

**Grammar and voice**
- Active voice, present tense.
- Contractions are encouraged ("you'll", "can't").
- "You" and "your" for the reader; "we" for Atlassian. No gendered pronouns for unknown people; use "they".
- US English spelling.

**Headings**
- Sentence case, no period, no bold or italics. Avoid question marks; rephrase as a statement.

**Lists**
- Six items or fewer; split longer lists.
- Parallel phrasing. Fragment bullets start lowercase with no end period; full-sentence bullets are capitalized and end with a period.
- Numbered lists only for ordered steps.

**Numbers**
- Digits for most numbers ("8 characters"). Spell out a number that starts a sentence; in long-form copy spell out one through nine.
- Ranges with "to", not a dash ("2 to 4 minutes"). "Step 1 of 3", not "1/3". Commas from 1,000.

**Punctuation**
- Oxford comma.
- Exclamation marks: at most one per asset, and ideally none.
- Dashes sparingly; prefer rewriting the sentence. Hyphenate compound modifiers ("team-wide update").
- Ellipsis character (…) with no spaces.
- Double quotes for quotes; never quotation marks for emphasis.

**Inclusive writing:** follow https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/inclusive-writing.

## Kit-specific rules (ours, not Atlassian's)

- Only state facts in `framework/messaging-framework.md` or the brief. Name gaps instead of filling them.
- Every asset carries the footer from `framework/messaging-framework.md`: these are unofficial concept assets, not Atlassian announcements.
- Banned: revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, synergy, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, unleash, supercharge, "single pane of glass".
