# Style references: real Loom launch content

Real published examples the writer can study for structure and register. Link only; don't copy text into assets.

| Asset type | Example | What to notice |
|---|---|---|
| Blog post (feature launch) | [Let Loom AI do the work: AI workflows](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/loom/loom-ai-workflows) | Headline promises an outcome; a 3-step how-it-works; use cases by team (bug reports, process docs, reviews); CTA at top, middle and end; about a 3-minute read. Note it leans enthusiastic ("supercharge") further than the voice rules allow; the kit should stay plainer. |
| Blog post (major launch) | [Welcome to the next era of Loom](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/loom/loom-ai-launch) | Big-picture framing before features. |
| Blog post (product refresh) | [Meet the all-new Loom for Mac](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/loom/all-new-loom-for-mac) | Short, benefit-led sections. |
| Pricing and plans (facts) | [Loom pricing](https://www.atlassian.com/software/loom/pricing) | Plan names and what each includes. |
| Feature reference (facts) | [Loom AI features](https://support.atlassian.com/loom/docs/loom-ai-features/) | Exact feature names and which plans get them. |

To add for the other assets (the PMM can pick): a Loom release notes page, a Loom email announcement, Loom social posts on LinkedIn and X, and an Atlassian sales one-pager or datasheet.
